# 🎬 Digital Amnik — Founder's Video Script
### For: Landing Page Hero Section
### Duration: ~2.5 – 3 minutes
### Tone: Confident, conversational, authentic, urgent

---

## PRE-PRODUCTION NOTES

**Setting:** Clean, professional background (dark wall or branded backdrop with "Digital Amnik" logo). Good lighting — a ring light or softbox. Dress professionally but approachable (smart casual: button-up shirt, no tie).

**Camera:** Direct eye contact with camera. Shoot in 1080p or 4K. Landscape orientation (16:9).

**Style Reference:** Conversational, like talking to a friend who's a real estate agent. NOT a corporate ad. Passionate, real, with energy.

## 📌 HOW TO ADD VIDEO TO THE LANDING PAGE

After you record and upload the video:

1. Upload to **YouTube** (set visibility to **Unlisted** so it only plays on your site)
2. Copy the video ID from the URL: `youtube.com/watch?v=`**`XXXXXXXXXX`**
3. Open `index.html`
4. Find this line:
   ```html
   data-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/YOUR_VIDEO_ID?autoplay=1&rel=0"
   ```
5. Replace `YOUR_VIDEO_ID` with your actual YouTube video ID
6. Save and upload — the video will now play when visitors click the ▶ button!
