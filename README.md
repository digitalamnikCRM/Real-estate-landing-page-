- **Deep Emerald & Forest Gold Luxury Aesthetic:** Architecturally inspired bespoke dark theme (`#040907`, `#10B981`, gold accents).
- **The Number Game (Visual ROI Breakdown):** Transparent real estate math showing how a ₹1L/month investment yields 300X+ ROI on booked inventory.
- **Client Video Reviews:** Interactive video review cards with glowing play buttons for developer video testimonials.
- **Client PDF Case Studies:** Documented blueprints & downloadable PDF reports for Tier-1 high-rises, villa townships, and commercial hubs.
- **Meet the Founder:** Executive founder note and portrait for Amaan Khan, plus an industry leader photo gallery.
- **90-Day Performance Guarantee:** Clear performance commitments and verified buyer qualification metrics.
- **Professional Real Estate FAQs:** Interactive accordion answering key questions for developers and promoters.
- **Unified CTA Architecture:** Standardized `Book Free Project Audit` buttons throughout all sections.
